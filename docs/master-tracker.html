The critical path

Vertical phases are integration checkpoints — prove the whole stack on a bigger target. Horizontal tracks are the reusable capabilities you build once and apply everywhere. The spine is linear; the tracks feed it continuously.

F foundations→ P0 reliability→ P1 C library→ P2 scale→ P3 C++→ P4 embedded→ P5 ArduPilot→ P6 autonomy

Tracks SEM · VER · MODEL · IDIOM · PERF · INFRA run alongside all of it. The nearest fundable milestone is the P1 exit: an unseen real C library, converted hands-off, verified, under 5% refusal.

Caveat · physics "Perfect safe Rust for all of ArduPilot" is unachievable as stated — register access, DMA, and ISRs are irreducibly unsafe. The real deliverable is verified safe Rust for the algorithmic layers + a thin, audited, clearly-marked unsafe hardware shim.

Caveat · research Items tagged research-hard are open problems, not schedule you can burn down on weekends. And the whole ladder rests on the reliability floor (P0) holding — if it slips, everything above it slips too.
